# Policy — UAN Dual Employment Stop Rule

**Owner:** HR Operations · **Applies to:** all permanent and contractor hires (not fresher, campus or intern — see exemptions)

## The rule

Before an offer letter is released, the candidate's UAN (Universal Account Number) service history must be checked for overlapping employment windows. If the UAN shows the candidate as active with a second employer during a period that overlaps their declared current or most recent employment, **the offer is not released.**

This is a **stop**, not a slow-down. No level of hiring urgency, manager appetite, or business pressure authorizes releasing the offer while the flag is open.

## What counts as an overlap

Two or more UAN-linked employment records whose date ranges intersect by any amount. A gap between jobs is not a violation. An overlap — even a few weeks — is.

## What happens when the stop triggers

1. Rashmi flags the case and does not proceed with offer generation.
2. Recruiter is notified with the specific overlap dates.
3. Recruiter raises it with the candidate — sometimes there's a legitimate explanation (data entry error at a previous employer, a short client-site secondment recorded oddly).
4. If the candidate cannot resolve it, the case escalates: Rashmi → Recruiter → Hiring Manager → Rajesh Kumar / Narang for a deviation decision.
5. Deviation approval, if granted, is documented and attached to the candidate file. Absent that, the offer is rejected.

## Exemptions

Freshers, campus hires and interns are exempt — no prior employment history is expected, so UAN service history is not applicable.

## Why this exists

Dual employment carries statutory and compliance exposure tied to EPFO reporting, separate from any internal HR judgment about the candidate. This is the one rule in the Offer Desk process that is regulatory in nature, not internal policy (see the Function Head interview, Q2).
