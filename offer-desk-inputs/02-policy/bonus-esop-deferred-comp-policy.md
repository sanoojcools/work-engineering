# Policy — Joining Bonus, Deferred Bonus, ESOP

## Joining bonus

- Roughly 40–50% of permanent candidates receive a joining bonus, at the hiring manager's discretion within the salary approval.
- Amounts **greater than ₹1,00,000** are split into two installments, paid in the 4th and 8th month of employment.
- Amounts **at or below ₹1,00,000** are paid as a single installment in the 4th month.

## Deferred bonus

- Folded into the CTC at offer stage, but vests on the first anniversary of the Date of Joining (DOJ), not the offer date.
- Vest date must be entered manually on the offer letter and in the payroll report — this is the single most error-prone manual field in the process (per the Offer Desk sitting).

## ESOP

- Eligible only at **Grade 7 and above**, or for candidates in a named leadership track regardless of grade.
- ESOP clause is added to the offer letter template automatically once grade/track eligibility is confirmed in Zwayam.

## Variable pay

- **GTS and Core, Grade 5 and above:** 8% variable pay is mandatory.
- **Platforms, any grade:** no variable pay at any level. If a salary approval mail lists variable pay for a Platforms candidate, this is a data error and must be flagged to the recruiter before the offer is generated — not silently corrected.
