# Job Description — Offer Desk SME (Offer Management)

**Title:** HR Operations Specialist, Offer Desk
**Reports to:** Head of HR Operations
**Locations covered:** Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad
**Incumbent (this batch):** Rashmi KN

## Purpose

Own the offer-release process from salary approval to signed offer letter, for every hire type (permanent, contractor, campus, fresher, intern) across GTS, Platforms and Core functions.

## Accountable for

- Verifying all required documents are uploaded and complete before an offer is released, per the hire-type checklist.
- Cross-checking salary approval against the current salary grid and escalating any deviation for 2-level approval.
- Applying the dual-employment stop rule against UAN service history without exception, appetite or manager pressure.
- Triggering offer letter generation and monitoring the Zoho signing chain through to candidate signature.
- Maintaining the Master Joining Sheet, PR sheet (contractors), email-ID tracker and payroll report inputs.

## Explicitly NOT accountable for

- Final hiring decisions — made upstream by the hiring manager and recruiter.
- Deviation approval itself — Rashmi flags and routes it; Vasu and Nagaraj approve it.
- Onboarding after Day 1 — handed off to Onboarding SPOC once the acknowledgement is signed.

## Escalation path

Rashmi → Recruiter → Hiring Manager → Rajesh Kumar / Narang (dual-employment and other stop-rule exceptions only).

## What "done" looks like for one candidate

Documents verified complete (or the offer is blocked with a named reason), salary within grid or deviation approved, offer letter signed by TA Head then candidate, onboarding acknowledgement signed, candidate handed to Onboarding SPOC.
