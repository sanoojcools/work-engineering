# VERDICT's uncapped mean-to-level bands are our design, not V8's

V8 specifies the four hard gates and the seven scored properties, but not the arithmetic that turns the mean of those seven scores into an uncapped recommended level. We chose mean-of-seven with band cutoffs (`<2`→L1 … else L5, L6 only with determinism=5, verifiability≥4, tacitness≥4, mean≥4.5). Treat this function as replaceable — it's the one piece of the VERDICT derivation open to recalibration without contradicting the spec.
